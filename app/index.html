Measured results

Every number here is computed by the scripts in the repo from NASA data, not typed in.

Where the data comes from

  • Ground shape and photo: USGS merged maps of Gale Crater from orbit (HiRISE, CTX, HRSC): heights every 1 m, photo at 25 cm.
  • Curiosity's path and stops: NASA's rover location feed (sol, site, drive, position).
  • Weather: Curiosity's REMS station, one record per sol.
  • Radiation: Curiosity's RAD instrument, about 0.64 mSv per sol on the surface.
  • Close-ups: Curiosity Navcam stereo photos at each stop, gaps filled by Depth Anything V2.

How the route is chosen

  • The ground is split into 2 m squares. Any square steeper than your limit is off-limits.
  • Walking speed changes with grade (Tobler's hiking rule), set to 3 km/h on flat ground in a suit.
  • The planner (A* search) finds the quickest path between your two taps.

What's assumed

  • Speed and slope limits are example values, not NASA EVA rules.
  • 1 m orbital heights miss rocks smaller than about 2 m; the Navcam close-up shows those.
  • Weather and radiation are what Curiosity measured on that sol, used as a forecast.
